Threads — the filing cabinet becomes a thinking record

Sessions cluster on-device (embedding similarity + shared checklists) into recurring idea-threads. A thread is led by its latest open question — the listener's own words are the index. “Continue this thread” resumes with the listener's memory seeded; “Ask your library” answers only in quotes of what you actually said, cited by session and timestamp. Memory without commentary: labels derive from recurrence, never from model opinion, and nothing ever pings you.

  • 4
    “Where you stopped” — the thread's open question, pinned. It is the resume point and the reason to come back; sessions where the listener never spoke show as “No question — it just listened”, worn proudly.
  • 5
    Continue this thread — the one filled button in the entire app. It starts a live session whose listener history is seeded with compact prior-thread context, open question first. The listener behaves identically (silence-first); its one earned question may now span sessions. The prompt forbids opening recaps — no “Welcome back!”.
  • 6
    The timeline is strung on questions, not files. “you picked this up on Jul 2” is computed from your own later words answering it — recurrence, not model judgment. Tap any node for the full session.
  • Escape hatches: a mis-clustered session can be dragged out of a thread; two threads can be merged. Clustering must stay legible and reversible (open question in README).
