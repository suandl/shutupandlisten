The live session — the waiting is the interface

One screen, three calm states, no chrome. The patience ring replaces the five-color debug taxonomy: it breathes while you talk, quietly fills during a pause, and dissolves the moment you resume — the machine visibly holding its tongue. The transcript is demoted to a one-line peek. When a thought finally lands, the screen itself goes quiet and the one earned question is staged alone. Running example: the reading-app idea from prompts/claude.md.

  • 1
    The patience ring is the whole screen. 2 px hairline track, breathing at 5.8 s — calm even when nothing happens. No state dots, no dB meter, no progress bar strip.
  • 2
    Voice is light, not text. A soft ember glow inside the ring answers the mic level — proof it's alive without streaming words at you. Machine states listening/talking collapse into this one word + glow.
  • 3
    Transcript peek: the last line only, right-anchored with a fade, so the eye can confirm capture and leave. Tap or drag up to expand (state 4). No streaming partials center-stage — that invites self-editing.
  • Two verbs only: pull a thread (invite the one question now — bypasses the gate's earned spacing) and end. Knobs, gear and checklist are gone from the live screen entirely.

Degraded, not broken: if the model is unreachable the only change is a quiet caption under the elapsed time — “offline — still recording; questions paused”. Never a modal over the silence.

  • 4
    State word, not state colors. “waiting it out” is the best copy in the current app, promoted from a footnote. It is the only text that changes. Same ember, lower voltage — no orange/purple semaphore.
  • 5
    The arc is the patience window — patienceProgress drawn as stroke offset, starting at 12 o'clock. When the EOU veto extends the window (trailing “and…”), the fill visibly slows; sub-floor breaths never move it at all.
  • Resume = dissolve. Speech resetting the window plays as the arc melting back over ~400 ms, not snapping. The loop shown here is the demo; live, fill duration comes from the actual deadline.
  • Declines are invisible. A window that closes and gets declined (gate says silence) just dissolves the arc — the thinker was never interrupted, so the UI shows nothing. No spinner, no “deciding” state.
