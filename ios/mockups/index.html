Silence is the product.
The design should look like it.

Four screens that need discussion before implementation, plus the identity that binds them. The engine already proves restraint; these mockups dress it as one: e-ink calm, not dashboard energy — generous negative space, a single ember accent, motion that breathes, dark-mode-first (people think out loud at night and on walks). Attitude in the name, serenity in the behavior. Rationale and open questions live in README.md.

The signature — the patience ring

One mark, three tenses

A 2 px hairline circle is the entire brand mark. It breathes while it listens (5.8 s cycle, ±3% scale), an ember arc fills clockwise while it waits out a pause — dissolving the instant you resume — and it settles to a single point when its one question arrives. The same grammar at every size: 284 pt session hero, 34 pt Live Activity, 22 pt Dynamic Island, 7 pt question marker in lists. Never a bar, never a spinner, never a second color.

Palette — night paper & ember

Dark is the designed experience; light is a faithful paper translation (toggle your OS appearance — this page and every mockup follow prefers-color-scheme). One accent, used only for what the listener does: the arc, the question, a citation. Everything else is warm ink.
