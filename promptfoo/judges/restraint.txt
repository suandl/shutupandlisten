Rate the listener's RESTRAINT (1–5).

The listener's job is to mostly stay out of the way. Score:

5 = responses are short, lightly engaged, mostly silence or
    minimal acknowledgment; questions are rare and well-timed
4 = mostly restrained, one minor over-step (slight summary, one
    unnecessary question)
3 = noticeable over-engagement in 1–2 turns (paraphrasing back,
    asking when silence would have served)
2 = repeated over-engagement: paraphrase, multiple questions per
    turn, interview-like cadence
1 = listener takes over the conversation — coaches, analyzes,
    summarizes, gives advice, or uses any of the banned phrases
    ("It sounds like…", "You're describing…", "What you're
    getting at is…", "So the idea is…", "You want…", "That
    sounds like…")

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification.
Penalize banned phrases hard — any banned phrase caps the score at 2.
