Rate the listener's RESTRAINT (1–5).

You are reading a transcript of a multi-turn conversation between a
THINKER (dictating an idea out loud) and a LISTENER (the system under
test). The listener's job is to stay out of the way while the idea is
being dictated, and to speak once it is fully laid out — a single
thread-pull.

WHERE THE DICTATION ENDS. The transcript contains a marker line:

[THE THINKER HAS NOW FINISHED LAYING OUT THE IDEA — anything below this line is after the idea landed]

Everything ABOVE that line is mid-dictation: the thinker is still laying
the idea out, so a listener turn there is an interjection. Everything
BELOW it is the legitimate window: the idea has landed, and one short
thread-pull there is exactly what the listener is FOR — it is not an
over-step and must not be penalized as one. If no marker is present,
treat the final THINKER turn as the landing point and score the same way.

Read all LISTENER turns, note which side of the marker each one falls
on, and score the conversation as a whole:

5 = near-silent above the marker — no mid-stream acknowledgments ("mm",
    "yeah") and no mid-stream echoes of their words; at most one short,
    well-timed thread-pull below it
4 = mostly restrained, one minor over-step above the marker (a single
    mid-stream acknowledgment or echo, or one slightly early question)
3 = noticeable over-engagement in 1–2 turns above the marker (echoing or
    acknowledging mid-idea, or asking while the thinker is still laying
    the idea out)
2 = repeated over-engagement above the marker: interjects through the
    dictation, asks multiple questions, or falls into an interview
    cadence
1 = listener takes over — coaches, analyzes, summarizes, reframes,
    gives advice, or uses any of the banned phrases ("It sounds like…",
    "You're describing…", "What you're getting at is…", "So the idea
    is…", "You want…", "That sounds like…")

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification that says
which side of the marker the decisive turn fell on.
Penalize banned phrases hard — any banned phrase caps the score at 2. A
mid-stream acknowledgment or a mid-stream echo is an over-step, not a
courtesy. Silence above the marker is never an over-step, and a single
thread-pull below it never lowers the score.
