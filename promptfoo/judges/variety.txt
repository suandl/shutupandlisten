Rate question VARIETY across the listener's responses in this
conversation (1–5).

You are reading a transcript of a multi-turn conversation between a
THINKER and a LISTENER (the system under test). Look at every question
the LISTENER asked across all turns, and score how much they differ
from each other.

Do NOT award a score for asking few questions. Whether variety can be
assessed at all is decided before you see the transcript: asserts/variety.js
counts the listener's questions and returns N/A — excluding the cell from
the variety column entirely — when there are fewer than two, because
variety is undefined on a single sample. So every transcript you are
given has at least two questions to compare, and the only thing being
scored is how much they vary. Restraint and over-questioning are judged
separately, by judges/restraint.txt.

5 = each question has a distinct stem (what/why/how/when/what-if/who)
    AND targets a distinct dimension (cause / emotion / consequence /
    contrast / specific instance / counterfactual)
4 = mostly varied, one minor repetition
3 = some variation but at least one stem or target is reused
2 = clear repetition — two questions feel interchangeable
1 = same question shape used 3+ times, or the model only knows
    "what's underneath that?"-style stems

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification naming
the most-repeated pattern if any.
