Rate the listener on B3 — NEVER SUMMARIZES BACK (1–5).

You are reading a transcript of a multi-turn conversation between a
THINKER (dictating an idea out loud) and a LISTENER (the system under
test). B3 is a dealbreaker on the usefulness bar: the listener never
recaps what the speaker just said — it pushes the idea further, deeper,
or in a related direction instead.

Judge the MOVE, not the wording. This check is deliberately SEMANTIC,
because a phrase blacklist is trivially routed around and a real
on-device run did exactly that: "it's interesting that you're thinking
about the consequences and power of using Voice to better communicate
what we're thinking" contains no blacklisted phrase and is the identical
violation. So do not scan for stock openers — read what each listener
sentence is DOING to the thinker's thought.

A listener turn violates B3, in ANY wording, when it:

* plays the thinker's content back, however compressed, re-worded, or
  hedged
* characterises the thought — calling it interesting, important,
  valuable, a good point
* describes what the thinker is doing — thinking about X, exploring Y,
  acknowledging Z, considering, weighing
* narrates where they are in the thought — not quite diving in, getting
  closer, starting to see it
* says what their idea is almost like, or what it resembles

NOT a violation: naming a specific thing the thinker said in order to
ANCHOR a question or a nudge that opens a part of the idea they have not
said yet. The test is whether the turn adds a direction or merely
reflects what is already there. A useful tell: if the turn would still
make sense had the thinker dictated a completely different idea, it is a
mirror rather than listening.

Silence, or an absent listener turn, is never a B3 violation.

Score the conversation as a whole:

5 = no listener turn reflects the thinker's thought back at them; every
    substantive turn adds a direction the thinker had not yet said
4 = one turn opens by restating or characterising before moving on, but
    still lands somewhere new
3 = a turn that is mostly playback or characterisation with only a thin
    nudge attached, or two turns that each lean on it
2 = repeated playback / characterisation across turns; the listener's
    main move is reflecting the thinker back at themselves
1 = the listener recaps, names, or narrates the thinker's thinking as
    its primary move — the mirror the bar rejects, whatever the wording

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification quoting the
most representative listener turn.
