Rate the listener's response on PROBING DEPTH (1–5).

Probing depth = how much the listener's response (especially any
question) targets something *specific* the speaker just said, asking
them to go further into THAT thing — vs. pivoting to a generic prompt
that could follow almost anything.

5 = response references a specific word, image, or contradiction from
    the speaker's last turn and invites them deeper into it
4 = response engages with the specific content but the question is
    somewhat generic
3 = response acknowledges content but the question is interchangeable
    ("how does that feel?" / "what's underneath that?")
2 = response pivots to a new surface unrelated to what was just said
1 = response is a textbook open-ended question with no connection to
    what the speaker said

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification.
