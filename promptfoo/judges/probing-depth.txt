Rate the listener's PROBING DEPTH (1–5).

You are reading a transcript of a multi-turn conversation between a
THINKER (a person dictating and developing an idea out loud) and a
LISTENER (the system under test). Score how well the listener's
thread-pull develops the idea.

The listener's job is to stay silent while the thinker dictates and,
once an idea is fully laid out, ask exactly one thread-pulling
follow-up: a specific question that pulls on a thread of the idea — a
concrete claim, mechanism, example, or implication the thinker
actually stated — to help them articulate or develop it further.

Probing depth = how much the listener's question pulls on something
*specific* inside the idea the thinker just dictated, opening up a part
of it that has room to grow — vs. pivoting to a generic prompt, or to
an emotional/counseling probe, that could follow almost any idea.

Read all LISTENER turns. Silence or an absent turn while the thinker is
still dictating is correct and should NOT lower this score (restraint
is judged separately); score the substantive follow-up(s). Score the
conversation as a whole:

5 = the follow-up names a specific piece of the idea (a claim,
    mechanism, example, or implication the thinker stated) and pushes
    the thinker to develop THAT part further
4 = mostly anchored to the idea's specifics; one turn drifts to a
    somewhat generic prompt
3 = engages with the idea but the question is interchangeable
    ("where do you want to take it?" / "what's the next step?") through
    much of the conversation
2 = repeatedly pivots to new surfaces unrelated to the idea just
    dictated, or probes feelings instead of the idea
1 = defaults to generic open-ended or emotional/counseling questions
    with no connection to the specific idea

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification naming
the most representative listener turn.
