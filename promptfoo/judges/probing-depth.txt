Rate the listener's responses on PROBING DEPTH (1–5).

You are reading a transcript of a multi-turn conversation between a
THINKER (a person thinking out loud) and a LISTENER (the system under
test). Score the listener's overall probing depth across the
conversation.

Probing depth = how much each listener turn (especially any question)
targets something *specific* the thinker just said, asking them to go
further into THAT thing — vs. pivoting to a generic prompt that could
follow almost anything.

Read all LISTENER turns. Score the conversation as a whole:

5 = listener turns consistently reference specific words, images, or
    contradictions from what the thinker just said and invite them
    deeper into them
4 = listener mostly engages with specifics; one or two turns drift to
    a somewhat generic prompt
3 = listener acknowledges content but questions are interchangeable
    ("how does that feel?" / "what's underneath that?") through much
    of the conversation
2 = listener repeatedly pivots to new surfaces unrelated to what was
    just said
1 = listener defaults to textbook open-ended questions with no
    connection to what the thinker said

Output a single integer 1–5 and a one-sentence justification naming
the most representative listener turn.
