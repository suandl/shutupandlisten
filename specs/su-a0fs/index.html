shutupandlisten.sh · site mockups · August 2026

Silence is the product.
The site should look like it.

Three mockups of the public site, all executing one visual direction — the identity already shipped for the iOS app, extended to the web. Not three ideas: one idea at three settings, so the comparison isolates real choices rather than taste. Rationale per mockup and the open questions live in README.md.

The bet is contrast, not volume. In a feed of loud AI landing pages a near-black page that does almost nothing is the pattern interrupt — so every version pushes the identity to a confident extreme rather than dressing it down. The failure mode to avoid is timid, not loud.

Negative space Maximum. A full viewport with one mark in it; 34 vh between blocks. Generous but composed. The fold is full-bleed type, then a normal editorial rhythm. Moderate. The page carries a transcript, so space is spent inside it, not around it.

Risk if it's wrong Reads as unfinished rather than composed; bounce before the scroll. Reads as an ad; the claim has to carry with no proof under it. Reads as documentation; the fold is doing work, not stopping traffic.

What carries over from the app

One accent, ever

Ember is reserved for what the listener does — the patience arc, its one question. Everything else is warm ink on near-black. No second colour, no state taxonomy, no gradient.

Ember here is sulAccent verbatim — Color(red:0.91, green:0.67, blue:0.36) — the value shipped in ios/App/UI/Accent.swift. This is the canonical ember: ios/mockups/ was repointed to match it, so there is nothing left to reconcile.

One mark, three tenses

A hairline circle is the entire brand mark. It breathes while it listens, an ember arc fills clockwise while it waits out a pause — dissolving the instant you resume — and it settles to a single point when its one question arrives. Never a bar, never a spinner.

Type is the app's ramp unchanged — question 24/1.42 regular, caps at 11 px with 20% tracking for state words, SF Mono for every time and filename so they read as instrument markings. Nothing in this product is bold-shouted, including the loud mockup: line's 104 px hook is regular weight. Size carries the conviction; weight would only carry volume.

How to read these

  • Open hold.html, line.html, cadence.html in any browser. No server, no build step, no external requests — every glyph, mark and colour is in the file.
  • Dark is the designed experience. Switch your OS appearance to see the light translation (prefers-color-scheme); it is a faithful paper version, not a second design.
  • The provider selector is live in all three — click Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini and the excerpt, the filename, the line count and the paste instructions all change. It is CSS only; there is no script on any page.
  • The copy buttons toggle to their confirmed state so you can judge that moment. On the real site that is the one place a few lines of clipboard JS are unavoidable.
  • Each page carries a small annotation card, bottom right. That is mockup furniture — not part of the design.
  • Picking the winner is not this bead's job. These exist so that choice can be made by looking.
