# Usefulness Bar — on-device quiet companion

Status: ratified by the operator 2026-06-29 (unit U1, epic su-lou).

## What this is

The **usefulness bar** is the on-device acceptance reference for the quiet
thought companion. It replaces a scored comparison to **the baseline** (the
operator's ChatGPT-voice plus instructions): the question is **"useful enough
to reach for, flaws and all?"** — not "better than the baseline?". It is the
acceptance reference for U6 (the warmed end-to-end loop) and U8 (the
recommendation), not a scored benchmark.

The bar is a short rubric of named, checkable behaviors, derived from the
operator's daily use of the baseline and tiered by weight.

## The bar

### Core — dealbreakers

A failure on any core item means the operator will not reach for the flow.

- **B1 — Holds silence through an unfinished thought.** A thinking-pause is
  never treated as a turn; no listener turn lands mid-thought. Interrupting an
  unfinished thought is the cardinal failure.
- **B2 — Yields the floor instantly.** If the speaker keeps talking, the
  companion backs off, keeps listening and transcribing, and folds the moment
  into a later, more valuable interjection. The speaker never has to talk over
  it to reclaim the floor.
- **B3 — Never summarizes back.** No recap of what the speaker just said; the
  move pushes the idea further, deeper, or in a related direction instead.

### Strong — turns tolerable into genuinely useful

- **B4 — Restraint: rare and brief.** Most pauses get nothing. When the
  companion does speak, it is a solid point in a sentence or two, not a
  paragraph.
- **B5 — Specific over generic.** A follow-up engages the specific things the
  speaker actually said, not a generic template stem (for example, avoid "when
  did you first notice this").

### Secondary — raises quality, can work without

- **B6 — Feels real, not robotic.** Tone and voice read as alive. Linked to B5:
  generic content reads as robotic, so specificity carries much of this. Strong
  voice production is desirable but not required — timing and content matter
  more than tone.
